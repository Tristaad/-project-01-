Top 5 Things I Enjoy

    I like to play a lot of games, from games on my phone, to games on my xbox and pc. Generally, I play on my pc, mostly because when i get done with doing my classwork, I am already on my pc since I do classes online.

    I enjoy readng quite a bit, and when I do read, it is usually fiction, and most of the books I actually own are from Rick Riordan's mythology series. Generally, I just enjoy fantasy a lot.

  3. Making Art

    I really enjoy making art, whether it be with a pencil in the margins of a notebook, or digitally on my pc. I actually intended to go to college for digital art, but was never able to find any classes or degrees that would work for it near me. It is a good thing I enjoy coding as a form of art too.

  4. Singing Music

    I legitimately love to belt out music at the top of my lungs. A lot of the songs I love to sing are the classic ones from my childhood, like fireflies or the lazy song, and if you were nearby when I am in the shower, you'd probably hear me singing out loud.

  5. Hanging out with Friends

    I enjoy spending time with my online friends. I actually don't have any friends that live nearby, and I have to stay at home to take care of things around here, so I really enjoy the time I spend with my friends online, whether it be just chatting, or playing a game together.
