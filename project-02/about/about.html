About Me

My name is Tristan Adame, and I am a Digital Media and Professional Communications student at Texas Tech University. At the time of making this portfolio, I am 22 years old. I am interested in work that combines creativity, technology, communication, and hands-on problem solving.

Background

My entire life, I have loved to create. As I got older, my interests have gradually moved toward digital creation and technical work. I enjoy learning how things are built, experimenting with different approaches, and improving projects over time rather than expecting everything to work perfectly the first time, though it does feel great when it does.

Interests

Some of my interests include web design, digital art, 3D modeling, 3D printing, video games, and other creative projects. I especially enjoy projects where I can design something digitally and eventually turn it into something useful or physical. While they are not always the most polished or useful, I do enjoy making gadgets and knick-knacks for my parents.
